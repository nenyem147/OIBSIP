Project Overview
Lady Liberty is a static website designed to showcase the story of the Statue of Liberty. 
It features a visually appealing layout, historical timeline, inspirational quote, and symbolic representations of what the monument stands for.

Tech Stack
HTML5 – For the structure and content of the website.
CSS3 – For styling, layout, and visual presentation.
Google Fonts – For elegant typography using Playfair Display and Poppins.
Wikimedia Commons – For the Statue of Liberty image.

Features
Hero Section: An attractive introduction featuring the Statue of Liberty and a call-to-action button.
About Section: Provides information about the monument, its origin, design, and significance.
Historical Timeline: Highlights important events in the history of the Statue of Liberty, from its original concept in 1865 to its centennial celebration in 1986.
Inspirational Quote: Features a famous excerpt from Emma Lazarus's poem The New Colossus.
Symbolism Section: Explores three important values represented by the monument:
1.Freedom
2.Hope
3.Unity
Navigation Bar: Allows visitors to easily navigate between website sections.
Footer Section: Includes the project title and copyright information.

The website combines elegant typography, modern styling, and structured content to create an engaging browsing 
experience.
